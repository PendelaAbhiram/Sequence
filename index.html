The process

Three steps back to its owner

No middleman, no shipping labels — just a clear trail from the sidewalk where it was found to the hands it belongs in.

01

Post the details

Snap a photo, drop a location, and describe what makes it recognizable. Takes about 60 seconds.

→
02

We surface the match

Relief checks new posts against the board automatically and flags likely matches to both sides.

→
03

Message and meet

Chat through the app to confirm details, then arrange a safe handoff at a public spot nearby.
